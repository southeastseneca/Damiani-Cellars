About

Damiani Wine Cellars

Damiani Wine Cellars is one of the most celebrated and consistently excellent wineries on the eastern shore of Seneca Lake — a premium producer founded in 2004 by long-time friends Lou Damiani and Phil Davis, built on a simple but powerful maxim: "Premium Without Pretense." Situated in Burdett just minutes north of Watkins Glen, Damiani has earned a reputation as a premier producer of world-class dry red wines in the Finger Lakes, alongside outstanding whites and a sparkling wine program that rivals anything on the East Coast.

The vineyards sit in the so-called "Banana Belt" on the southeastern shores of Seneca Lake — a microclimate of warmer temperatures, abundant sunlight, and an extended growing season uniquely suited to red grape cultivation. Under the guidance of winemaker Katey Larwood, whose journey spans California, Australia, New Zealand, France, and finally the Finger Lakes, Damiani continues to push the quality ceiling for what the region can produce. Minimal intervention, maximal expression of the vineyard — wines that you don't just taste, you feel.

The tasting room offers breathtaking views of Seneca Lake from the balcony and patio — widely regarded as some of the best views on the trail. Walk-in tastings welcome for groups of six or less. Private VIP tastings offer an in-depth guided experience with current releases, library wines, vertical tastings, and charcuterie pairings. On-site vacation rentals and The Cellar event space round out one of the most complete estate experiences on Seneca Lake.

In Our Words

Our Story

Damiani is the winery on the east shore that consistently silences skeptics about Finger Lakes reds. The Cabernet Franc is world-class. The Merlot and Lemberger are as good as anything in the region. And the sparkling program — award-winning Bruts that genuinely hold their own against the best of France and the West Coast — is something most visitors don't expect and immediately become fans of.

The balcony and patio view over Seneca Lake is legitimately one of the best on the entire trail. Add a game of bocce, a private VIP tasting with Katey or one of the wine educators, and the option to stay in one of their on-site vacation rentals — Damiani delivers a complete experience that earns the "Premium Without Pretense" tagline in every possible way.

— South East Seneca

The Wines

What's Pouring

World-class dry reds are the house specialty, alongside whites with genuine character and award-winning sparkling. Winemaker Katey Larwood brings global experience to Seneca Lake terroir.

  • Cabernet Franc ★The flagship red — consistently one of the best Cab Francs produced in New York State
  • Merlot ★Rich, plummy, and age-worthy — a premier Finger Lakes red from the Banana Belt
  • Lemberger ★Spiced, earthy, and expressive — a Finger Lakes specialty done with exceptional precision
  • Brut Sparkling ★Award-winning — rivals the best of France and the West Coast; an unexpected standout
  • GewürztraminerAromatic and expressive — floral, lychee, and spiced in the classic Alsatian style
  • ChardonnayBalanced and site-expressive — a white with genuine character from the estate
  • RieslingBright and mineral — classic east shore Seneca Lake expression
  • Library & Reserve WinesAvailable in VIP tastings — verticals and library pours from 20 years of DWC history

Full wine list at damianiwinecellars.com — wine club available for priority access to new releases.

Plan Your Visit

Tips for Visitors

  • Lead with the reds — the Cab Franc, Merlot, and Lemberger are why Damiani has the reputation it does. Don't skip them.
  • Try the sparkling — the Brut is genuinely exceptional and surprises almost everyone who tries it.
  • Sit on the balcony — the Seneca Lake view from the Damiani patio is one of the best on the entire trail.
  • Book a VIP tasting for groups of 2–16 — the library wine and vertical options are a special experience worth reserving.
  • Walk-ins welcome for groups of 6 or fewer — no reservation needed for a standard tasting or glass on the patio.
  • 4704 Route 414, Burdett — just 5 minutes north of Watkins Glen, one of the most convenient stops on the east shore.
